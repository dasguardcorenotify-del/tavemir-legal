Privacy Policy

Approved public policy: Tavemir stores the finance records you create on your device, requires no account and does not operate an app backend that collects those records. This policy is effective in Türkiye from 26 August 2026.

1. Publisher and contact

Tavemir is published and controlled by TURGAY YÜKSEL in Türkiye. Privacy and support enquiries can be sent to dasguardcore.notify@gmail.com.

2. Data used on your device

Tavemir may process the following information locally to provide the features you choose to use:

DataPurpose
Budgets and categoriesCompare monthly category limits with your recorded spending.
Accounts and transactionsCalculate balances, income, expenses, cash flow and category summaries.
Reminders and datesTrack bills, rent, subscriptions, cards, loans and vehicle obligations.
Debts, receivables and business contactsOrganize records and linked due dates you enter.
Preferences and app-lock valuesRemember language, currency, theme and protect app access.

3. Collection and sharing

Tavemir has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, required cloud account, bank connection or Tavemir-operated sync server. The app does not automatically transmit your finance records to the publisher.

Data can leave your device when you explicitly share a PDF, CSV, text or backup file. The destination you select then processes that copy under its own terms. Google Play separately processes store installation and diagnostic information under Google's policies; Tavemir does not receive an in-app analytics feed from that service.

4. Permissions and device services

  • Notifications: used to show reminders you schedule. Android marks reminder content private and can substitute generic lock-screen text when your system privacy setting hides sensitive content.
  • Boot completed: used to restore locally scheduled reminders after a device restart or app update.
  • Biometrics: when enabled, authentication is performed by the operating system; Tavemir does not receive your biometric template.

Tavemir does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera, phone, SMS or advertising permissions in its Android release manifest.

5. Security and backup

The optional PIN is stored as a salted, computationally expensive derived value rather than plain text. When launch locking is enabled, Tavemir also re-locks after 30 seconds in the background. This protects app access; it does not encrypt the underlying local records or exported files. Android system backup is disabled for Tavemir app data. Files you explicitly export are readable by anyone or any service you share them with, so choose destinations carefully.

6. Retention, correction and deletion

Local records remain until you edit or delete them, clear Tavemir's app data, or uninstall the app. Tavemir has no server account to delete. A file you exported must be deleted from its destination separately; Tavemir cannot remove copies held by another app or service.

7. Children

Tavemir is a general personal-finance organization tool and is not designed or marketed for children. The app does not knowingly collect children's data through a Tavemir backend because no such backend exists.

8. Your rights

You control local records directly in the app. For privacy questions or rights that cannot be exercised locally, contact the publisher at the address above. Applicable rights depend on your location.

9. Changes

If Tavemir's data practices or features materially change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure must be updated before the changed release is distributed. This policy was last updated on 26 August 2026.

Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

Does Tavemir connect to my bank?

No. Tavemir does not provide bank sync, open banking, payments or money transfer. You control the records you enter.

Where is my data stored?

Your app records are stored locally on your device by default. Tavemir has no required account or Tavemir-operated cloud sync backend.

Does Tavemir show ads or track me?

The audited v1 release contains no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK. Store services may independently process installation or diagnostics under their own terms.

What is included on Free and Premium?

Free keeps unlimited transactions, accounts, core tracking, portable JSON backup and business CSV, with useful limits of 2 active advanced budgets, 5 active advanced reminders, 1 active advanced credit card, and 2 active loans or debts. Premium removes those depth limits and adds advanced insights, Premium PDF reporting and five exclusive app icons. Tavemir remains ad-free on both plans.

How are Premium purchases billed?

Google Play sells and bills monthly, yearly and non-consumable Lifetime products. Tavemir shows the localized live Play price and never collects a card number. Subscriptions renew until canceled in Google Play. Restore purchases checks current Play ownership using the purchasing Google account.

What happens if Premium ends?

Your finance records are not deleted or hidden. You can still view, edit and delete existing records and use Free-supported exports. Only creation of new Premium-depth records returns to the Free limits.

Which currencies are supported?

Tavemir supports TRY, USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, AUD, CHF and BRL. It keeps currencies separate and does not invent exchange rates.

How do monthly budgets work?

You set a limit for an expense category, month and currency. Tavemir compares it with matching transactions and shows clear progress thresholds.

Will reminders always fire at an exact second?

No app can guarantee delivery on every Android device. Tavemir schedules local reminders and reports scheduling or permission failures, while the operating system and vendor battery rules retain final control.

Can I export my records?

Yes. Portable JSON backup and business CSV remain available on Free; Premium adds finance PDF reporting. Files leave the app only when you choose to share them. Purchase metadata is not inserted into finance backups.

Is the PIN encrypted storage?

No. The PIN protects entry to Tavemir and is stored as a hardened derived value, but it does not encrypt every local record or exported file.

Is Tavemir financial advice?

No. Tavemir is a personal record-keeping and organization tool, not a bank, lender, payment service, investment platform or adviser.

Who publishes Tavemir and how can I get support?

Tavemir is published by TURGAY YÜKSEL in Türkiye. The approved public support contact is dasguardcore.notify@gmail.com. This information is effective and last updated on 26 August 2026.

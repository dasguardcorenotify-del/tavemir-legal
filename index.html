Privacy is architecture

Your financial records stay on your device by default.

Tavemir has no app account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, bank sync, or Tavemir cloud backend. Data leaves the app only through an export or share action you start.

Read the privacy policy

What that means

  • No hidden cloud synchronization
  • No sale of personal finance data
  • Local notification scheduling
  • Explicit PDF, CSV and backup sharing
  • Android backup disabled for app data
  • Delete records in-app or remove all app data
